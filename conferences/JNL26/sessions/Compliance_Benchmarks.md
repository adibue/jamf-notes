## Jamf Platform API: Compliance Endpoints & Examples

- `/benchmarks`: Returns a list of benchmarks configured in Jamf Pro
- `/baselines`: Returns a list of baselines configured in Jamf Pro
- `/rules?baselineID=$BASELINE`: To return rules for chosen baseline
